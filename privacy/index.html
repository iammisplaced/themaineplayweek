Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

The Maine Playweek, LLC ("we") runs the showtimes website at showtimes.themaineplayweek.com and the Playweek apps for iPhone and Android. They show what's playing at cinemas across Maine. This policy explains what information they use, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version: you don't need an account, and the apps have no ads, analytics or tracking. Your location, watchlist and settings stay on your device. We don't sell or share personal information. The website uses Google Analytics to count visits.

The Playweek apps

Location

To show the theatres and showtimes nearest you, the apps can use your device's approximate location (never your precise location), but only if you allow it. You can enter a ZIP code instead, or neither. Your location is used on your device to sort theatres and work out distances. It is not sent to us.

  • Your device's location service provides your approximate location: Apple's on iPhone, and Google Play services on Android.
  • A ZIP code you enter is turned into a map position by Apple's geocoding service on iPhone, or by Zippopotam.us on Android. Only the ZIP code is sent.
  • The map comes from Apple Maps on iPhone and Google Maps on Android. To draw the map, those services receive the area being shown, which may be near you, along with your IP address.

You can turn off location access at any time in your device's settings.

Your watchlist and settings

Films you star, films you mark as seen or hidden, your saved location or ZIP code, and your other settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Film search

When you search for a film to add to your watchlist, what you type is sent to our server, which looks it up with The Movie Database (TMDb). We don't store your searches or link them to you. Results for a search may be cached so that the same search loads faster for everyone.

Notifications and widgets

If you allow notifications, the app checks for showtimes in the background and tells you when a starred film starts playing in Maine. This happens on your device. We don't run a notification server, and we don't receive a notification address for your device. Home-screen widgets use the same information already on your device.

Loading showtimes

The apps download showtimes, theatre details and film information from our database, which is hosted by Supabase, and film posters from TMDb. Like any internet request, these services see your device's IP address, and their standard server logs may record it. The apps don't send anything that identifies you.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone or Android settings, Apple or Google may share crash reports and basic usage statistics with us. These don't identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

The website

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they use. It sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location based on your IP address. See how Google uses this information. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies.
  • Location. As in the apps, you can share your approximate location through your browser or enter a ZIP code. Your location stays in your browser. Only a ZIP code is sent, to Zippopotam.us, to find its position.
  • Saved preferences. Your location choice, search radius, view and light or dark theme are saved in your browser's local storage, not on our servers. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.
  • Other services. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, film images from TMDb and post images from Substack. Those services receive your IP address. The site is hosted by Vercel, whose server logs may record IP addresses.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. The services named above handle data only as needed to provide what you asked for.

Children

The website and apps are for a general audience and aren't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
